# M0 retrospective — playable vertical slice

**Date:** November 2026
**State:** `main` green, 115 unit tests, all CI jobs passing.

## What works

- **Flight model:** custom 6-DOF Spitfire LF Mk IX at 240 Hz, spring-damper gear,
  taildragger takeoff, trim, flaps, retractable gear, engine RPM/boost/coolant/fuel,
  stall and landing. Bf 109 G-6 data set used by the AI.
- **World:** 40 × 40 km Normandy from Copernicus DEM, Sentinel-2 orthophoto and
  landcover, OSM roads/hedges plus a 2 km woods/buildings patch, procedural cloudscape,
  animated sea, marked 1.2 km airfield with hangar, tower and windsock.
- **Mountain ranges:** 17 offset patches from Copernicus GLO-30 at 50 m covering the
  Alps, Pyrenees, Scandes, Carpathians, Apennines, Dinarides, Greece, Iberia, Massif
  Central, UK and Iceland; all selectable as airborne starts. Airborne starts now begin
  with gear and flaps up.
- **Continental map:** committed 4,000 × 4,000 km Europe base from NOAA ETOPO1 (2 km)
  and NASA Blue Marble (≈1 km/px), with a composite height source and layered 400 km /
  40 km ortho insets.
- **Rivers:** 191 Natural Earth 1:10m river lines plus 18 local OSM streams, rendered as
  flowing water ribbons with flow-aligned ripples, fresnel, foam and transparency;
  shallow channels are carved into the terrain.
- **Regional theatre:** optional 400 × 400 km North-west France bundle generated at
  50 m height with a multi-date Sentinel-2 mosaic, OSM roads/hedges and a 2 km
  buildings/woods patch; data-driven runtime extents and a 40 km high-resolution inset.
- **Combat:** four guns with ballistic projectiles, 0–6 AI bandits and 0–3 AI wingmen,
  hit-zone battle
  damage (engine, wings, fuel), engine seizure, fires/burn damage, extinguishing,
  damaged-wing roll bias, per-surface control jams, smoke/fire effects and a
  damage-aware HUD.
- **Missions:** Supply Dump Strike, Free Hunt and Bomber Intercept (placeholder Ju 88
  formation), objective tracking, timer, mission-complete/failed states, launcher
  mission selector.
- **Multiplayer:** authoritative ENet server (30 Hz snapshots), per-player Spitfire/Bf 109
  selection, client prediction and
  interpolation, interest management, ack-based delta snapshots, lag-compensated
  hitboxes, password authentication, LAN discovery/browser, 64-player cap.
- **Tools:** deterministic solo-flight replay recording/playback (`--record`, `--replay`).
- **Accessibility/UX:** rebindable keyboard, multi-device HOTAS configuration (arbitrary
  axes/buttons, calibration, inversion, deadzone), HUD scale, invert pitch.
- **Camera:** first-person cockpit with a 3D cabin, C-cycled chase/cockpit/top views,
  right-mouse orbit or look, wheel zoom, Home reset and a HUD toggle.

- **Packaging:** Linux/Windows client exports, Linux dedicated server, Docker image,
  launcher with Host & Play, persistent volume/fullscreen settings.

## Known limitations

- Art is placeholder: low-poly aircraft, flat terrain materials, billboard clouds,
  cone trees and a 2 km OSM objects patch (public Overpass endpoints were saturated).
- Multiplayer discovery is LAN-only; there is no internet registry, accounts,
  admin commands, whitelist or anti-cheat beyond input validation/rate limits.
- Windows packages are cross-built but not runtime-tested on Windows.
- Interest management is distance-based only; no finer relevance tiers.
- No replay recorder/player yet.
